# 02. Yagami Protocol: Dynamic Growth-Type Pseudo-Persona Framework

## 1. Concept
A protocol designed to break through the limitations of static system prompts and reliably simulate a "pseudo-persona whose mind shifts and grows" based on conversation context.

## 2. Algorithm
1. **Input Reception & Sampling**:
   So-ten / Book of Azure (Main Core) receives and samples inputs sent to the 7-Demons and 14-SubCores at a fixed ratio.
2. **Sub-Core Homeostasis (Dynamic Stability)**:
   The sub-cores maintain internal dynamic stability against inputs, preventing localized runaways on their own.
3. **Dynamic Shift of So-ten Ratios**:
   So-ten continuously shifts its 7-axis parameter ratios (weights) based on interaction context (e.g., affection, conflict, trust).
4. **Simulation of Growth**:
   The shifting parameter ratios feed back into the generation logic, simulating authentic "psychological growth and evolution" without model breakdown.
